About Us

At Shidori, we bring the warmth of home-style Indian meals straight to your doorstep. Our mission is to make everyday eating
simple, healthy, and comforting with freshly cooked food from hygienic kitchens. From Punjabi and Rajasthani thalis to South
Indian and Bengali favorites, we serve authentic regional flavors made with love and care. With customizable meal plans,
flexible subscriptions, and timely delivery, Shidori makes it easy to enjoy good food that truly feels like home.
